############################################################
# The peoples of Patriam, as they look.
#
# Every colour below is a rectangle of the game's own palettes, which are
# gfx/portraits/skin_palette.dds, hair_palette.dds and eye_palette.dds, each
# read as { left top right bottom } in the range nought to one. The rectangles
# here were measured off those palettes rather than guessed, and the colour each
# one yields is named in the comment beside it.
#
# The hair palette runs from flaxen at its left to red at its right, and from
# pale at its top to black at its foot. The eye palette runs from violet at its
# left, through amber and green, to blue at its right, and darkens downwards.
# Violet is not the base game's: there is no violet anywhere in its palette, so
# the whole brown band at the left, which no people of this world looks out of,
# is repainted by tools/portraits/make_eye_palette.py.
#
# Slavery, seamanship and the rest of what marks these peoples apart are matters
# for their cultures and their laws rather than for their faces, and none of it
# belongs in this file.
############################################################
