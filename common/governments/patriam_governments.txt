############################################################
# Patriam: Rise of Empires. Governments.
#
# Three forms that Southern Kallonia holds in 6355 and that vanilla has no
# shape for. Everything else on the map is feudal, which is the fallback and
# needs no entry here.
#
# A house is the formal holdings of one family expressed as a realm, so its
# realm carries the family name rather than the name of the land.
# A city state is a single holding that answers to nobody, small in land and
# heavy in walls and coin.
# Ketan is neither a republic nor an ordinary kingdom. Its ruler inherits, but
# the seat is a trading city and the strength of it is the ledger rather than
# the levy.
############################################################
